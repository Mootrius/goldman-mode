<h1 align="center">Goldman Mode</h1>

<img src="docs/world-map.png" alt="The Goldman world map: locked areas in red, unlocked areas in green" width="100%">

<p align="center">Unlock Gielinor one area at a time by earning and spending Goldman GP!</p>

## How it works

1. Customize the plugin's settings to your personal preference
2. Earn Goldman GP via skilling, PvM, questing, and more
3. Strategize and decide which areas to unlock

## Area costs

There are 400+ hand-drawn areas built in to the plugin that are priced based on a combination of their size, usefulness, amenities, danger, and proximity to central locations. You can adjust the cost multiplier up or down to decide how hard you want the game-mode to be. The base cost of areas varies from thousands of Goldman GP to millions per area, and a full breakdown can be found [here](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/13WG-S-lcwAKKUWXfIKOT8Aw8vdFgFw1B1tN9dkwwMdU/edit?usp=sharing).

## GP payouts

You can earn Goldman GP via gaining experience, killing NPCs, gaining skill and combat levels, questing, completing diary tasks and CAs, and obtaining collection logs. You can adjust the payout multiplier up or down to decide how hard you want the game-mode to be. The base payout of GP varies generally with difficulty and rarity, and a full breakdown can be found [here](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/13WG-S-lcwAKKUWXfIKOT8Aw8vdFgFw1B1tN9dkwwMdU/edit?usp=sharing).
